What is included in a Rivaaj Kit?

Each Rivaaj Wedding Ritual Kit brings together the essential puja samagri and ritual items required for a specific ceremony, thoughtfully organised in one box.

The exact contents of every kit are clearly listed on the respective product page, along with anything that may need to be arranged separately.

Can the kit be customised for my Panditji’s list?

Yes. Our kits are developed with Pandit recommendations and around commonly followed North Indian Hindu wedding rituals and puja samagri requirements.

You can simply share the additional items or changes you need, and we’ll customise the kit wherever possible. We can also increase quantities of selected samagri items or help source any extra family- or Panditji-specific requirements, subject to availability.

How do I place an order?

Simply click “Order on WhatsApp” and share the kit(s) you need, your wedding or function date, delivery PIN code and any special requirements.

We’ll confirm the kit details, availability, customisation requirements, delivery timeline and complete order details with you before the order is finalised.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, Rivaaj Box offers pan-India delivery, subject to courier serviceability at your PIN code.

Share your delivery location while placing the order and we’ll confirm the expected dispatch and delivery timeline with you.

Are delivery charges included?

Delivery charges are additional and borne by the customer on an actual-cost basis.

Since package dimensions and weight can vary depending on the number and type of kits ordered, shipping charges depend on the box size, weight, destination PIN code and courier partner. The applicable cost will be shared with you on WhatsApp, and tracking details will be provided after dispatch.

How can I pay for my order?

Once your kit selection, customisation and delivery details are confirmed, our team will share the payment details and final payable amount with you on WhatsApp before processing your order.

This ensures that product, customisation and shipping charges are clearly communicated beforehand.

Can I change or cancel my order?

If you need to make a change or cancel an order, please contact us on WhatsApp as early as possible.

Whether a change or cancellation can be accommodated will depend on the stage of sourcing, customisation and packing of your order. Our team will confirm what is possible before proceeding.

What if an item is damaged, missing or looks different?

Some ritual essentials may be sourced in different batches, so the brand, colour, shape or packaging may occasionally differ from the product photographs, while we aim to maintain the intended quality and purpose of the item.

If an item arrives damaged, missing or incorrect, simply message us on WhatsApp with your order details and a clear photograph of the issue. Our team will review it and help with the quickest possible resolution.

We also recommend checking your kit soon after delivery and reviewing any family-specific requirements with your Panditji before the ceremony.

Are the kits travel-friendly for NRI or destination weddings?

Yes. Rivaaj Kits are designed to make carrying Hindu wedding puja samagri easier for NRI and destination weddings.

The essentials are thoughtfully organised and individually packed to help minimise mixing, leakage and contamination while keeping the kits practical to carry in your luggage. This makes it easier to celebrate your family’s customs and stay connected to your roots even when the wedding is away from home.

For international travel, we recommend checking your airline and destination-country regulations beforehand, particularly for powders, liquids, food items, seeds or other restricted materials.
